i created this site EACH ALL as an antidote to an internet that feels increasingly alienating through algorithmic content and performative constructions of digital identity. anyone is welcome to contribute images to the site in response to simple, open-ended prompts with the goal of creating a collective scrapbook of memories, ideas, and feelings. i will continue to add new prompts to the site so that the project can revitalize, shift, and become more capacious alongside its contributors. i firmly believe that the internet should be a playground rather than an anxious forum, and the only way we can transform our relationship to digital spaces is through cooperation beyond individual egos. this is a small experiment in sincerity, and i would love for you to be part of it.

—kevin cunanan chappelle :)
